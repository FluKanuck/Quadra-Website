Our inspectors will be presented daily with sites where they will be the eyes and ears of the field coordinator. The inspector reviews agreements, permits and reports, and thoroughly documents the day's activities and progress in the field. To do this, an inspector will be continuously uploading reports, photos and required documentation. We expect our inspectors to complete all their site visits in a safe, responsible and professional manner.

Knowledge of how to read industry agreements is a must.

Work for this position is based out of Hope, British Columbia, where there will be daily briefings and dispatch.

This role will work on pipeline inspections March through November, with a possibility of work as a utility locator through the winter in other locations.
