Our locators will be presented daily with sites where they will be challenged to find subsurface utilities. To do this, a locator will use electromagnetic (EM) locators and ground penetrating radar (GPR). As their training continues, locators will learn to use GPS surveying and other tools as our services and client demands grow. Our locators are trusted with their own assigned vehicles, for which they are responsible. We expect our locators to complete all their site visits in a safe, responsible and professional manner.

Our more experienced locators will work on more demanding sites with more challenging utility configurations. Pipeline inspections, pipeline protection, chamber inspections, utility line strike investigation reports and surveying are all aspects of our field that our experienced field hands are trusted with.
