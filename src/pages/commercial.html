72% of reported utility strikes in Canada are caused by contractors and developers.*

BC 1 Call can tell you where some utilities are located up to your property line. It does not include utilities on private property or inside buildings. That is the work we do.

When to get your utilities located

  • Before any ground disturbance

    Find out where your utilities are before starting new construction projects, excavation, or remediation, to accurately plan work and avoid line strikes.

  • Before purchasing a new property

    Find out if a property has an undisclosed oil tank, where a pipeline is located, or if you plan to build a new structure, where utilities are located.

  • After construction

    Document your as-built accurately with the true location of utilities for insurance documentation and easier future maintenance.
