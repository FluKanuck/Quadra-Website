BC 1 Call and private locates

What is BC 1 Call?

There is a vast network of underground pipes and cables used to transport oil and gas, water and sewage, electricity, and telecommunication services across British Columbia. Before you dig, find out what's buried below your site by requesting a locate from BC 1 Call.

BC 1 Call is the link between the excavating community and the owners of underground infrastructure. By contacting BC 1 Call, private landowners and commercial excavators reduce the risk of personal injury and property damage.

How to request a BC 1 Call locate

  1. Register as a BC 1 Call user and submit a locate request at bc1c.ca (or call 1-800-474-6886) with your contact information and details about the dig site.
  2. BC 1 Call identifies which of its members have underground infrastructure at or near your dig site, and notifies them of your request.
  3. You get same-day confirmation by email that your request was received, with the names of the members that were notified. You will also be advised to contact any other parties who are not members of BC 1 Call and may have underground facilities in your excavation area.
  4. The member companies respond within 3 business days with a map or drawing, or by marking the ground with paint or flags where their utilities are buried.

What BC 1 Call doesn't locate

BC 1 Call members locate their own lines, and most only go as far as your property line. Some send a map or drawing instead of marking the ground. Anything past the property line belongs to the property owner and isn't part of a BC 1 Call response. That includes:

  • Utilities on private property, between the property line and your building
  • Utilities inside buildings and concrete slabs
  • Buried oil tanks

If you're digging on private property, the BC 1 Call response is only part of the picture.
